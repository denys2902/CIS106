# Module 3 IPO Charts

## M3P1 - Stock Investment
| Input | Process | Output |
|---|---|---|
| Stock symbol, number of shares, cost per share | amount invested = shares * cost per share | Stock symbol, amount invested |

## M3P2 - Exam Points
| Input | Process | Output |
|---|---|---|
| Last name, midterm score, final exam score | total points = midterm * 0.40 + final * 0.60 | Last name, total exam points |

## M3P3 - Job Payment
| Input | Process | Output |
|---|---|---|
| Total job payment | payment for each person = total payment / 3 | Amount each person receives |

## M3P4 - Car Discount
| Input | Process | Output |
|---|---|---|
| Make, model, MSRP, discount decimal | amount off = MSRP * discount; discounted price = MSRP - amount off | Make, model, MSRP, discount percent, amount off, discounted price |

## M3P5 - Circle
| Input | Process | Output |
|---|---|---|
| Radius | area = 3.14 * radius * radius; perimeter = 2 * 3.14 * radius | Area, perimeter |
